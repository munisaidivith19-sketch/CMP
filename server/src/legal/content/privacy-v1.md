## 2. Information we collect

**Account and profile information** — name, title, gender, college e-mail address, role, department, year, section, semester, roll number or employee ID, designation, phone number, profile photo, and anything you add to your profile such as a bio, interests and skills.

**Academic and institutional records** — attendance, timetables, examination schedules, on-duty and leave requests, class assignments, study material access, achievements, club and event participation, and Skill Wallet projects and certificates (including NPTEL hall ticket numbers, which are not shown publicly).

**Campus services** — gate passes and outpasses, hostel and stay type, complaints, lost-and-found reports, SOS alerts, and transport assignments. For gate passes, the App stores a parent or guardian mobile number and sends a one-time code to it by SMS.

**Messages and community content** — chat messages, discussions, replies, announcements, comments and reports that you send or post.

**Location** — only in these situations:

- when a staff member starts a bus trip, that device shares its location until the trip ends, so students can see the bus;
- when you tap "Return to campus" on a gate pass, your location is checked once against the campus area;
- when you raise an SOS alert, the location your device provides is sent with it;
- "My location" on the bus map is shown only on your own device and is not sent to the server.

The App does not track your location in the background.

**Study Assistant** — the questions you ask and the answers given, saved as your conversations.

**Technical and security information** — IP address, browser or device type and name, sign-in times and outcomes, active sessions, push notification tokens, and error reports from the App (with personal details removed where possible).

**Policy acceptance** — the version of the Terms and Conditions you accepted and when.

## 3. Why we use it

- to provide the App's features and show you the information relevant to your role and class;
- to run College processes such as attendance, gate passes, transport, hostel, complaints and academic records;
- to send notifications you need, including push notifications;
- to keep accounts and the App secure, detect misuse and investigate incidents;
- to answer your questions in the Study Assistant;
- to meet the College's legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who can see it

- **Inside the College** — authorised staff see information according to their role and responsibilities (for example, a Class In-Charge sees their class). Other students see only what features are designed to share, such as your name in a class list or a chat.
- **Public** — nothing is public unless you choose to publish it, for example a Skill Wallet project you make public in the Innovation Gallery.
- **Service providers** that help run the App, only for that purpose: cloud hosting and database services, file storage, push notification services (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service), an SMS gateway for one-time codes, e-mail delivery, map and routing data providers, and an automated text-generation service that receives Study Assistant questions to produce answers.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account, academic and institutional records: for as long as you are associated with the College and afterwards as required by the College's record-keeping rules. **[TO BE SUPPLIED BY THE COLLEGE: retention period.]**
- Sign-in history: about 90 days. Security logs: about one year.

- Parent one-time code records: about 30 days.
- Study Assistant usage records: about 180 days.
- Policy acceptance records: kept as long as needed to show which version you accepted.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete information (you can edit parts of your profile yourself);
- erase information that is no longer needed, where the College is not required to keep it;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
- raise a grievance with the contact in section 1, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

Accounts are created and managed by the College. To close an account or ask for deletion, contact the College at the address in section 1. **[TO BE SUPPLIED BY THE COLLEGE: account closure procedure.]**

You can turn off push notifications in your device settings, but some features require them.

## 9. Security

We use access controls based on roles, encrypted connections, password hashing, session limits, automatic sign-out of idle web sessions, rate limiting and security monitoring to protect information. No system is completely secure; please keep your password private and report anything suspicious.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

The web app uses only cookies needed to keep you signed in and protect against cross-site request forgery. It also stores some preferences, such as the colour theme, in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. Each version has a version number and an effective date, and the current version is always available in the App and on the public website. When a change affects how we use your information in a significant way, we will tell you in the App.
