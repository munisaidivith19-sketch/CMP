## 4. Purposes of processing

Information is processed for these purposes:

- Provide App features and display information relevant to a user's role and class.
- Enable College academic and administrative processes.
- Manage attendance, examinations, assignments, gate passes, transport, hostel services and complaints.
- Facilitate communication between students, faculty and authorised staff.
- Send operational notifications and alerts.
- Maintain account and platform security, detect misuse and investigate incidents.
- Respond to questions submitted to the Study Assistant.
- Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

The Technical Provider processes information for providing and maintaining the agreed technical services, including troubleshooting, security, maintenance and infrastructure operations. Personal information is not sold by the Technical Provider or used by it for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes under this arrangement.

## 5. Institutional data and control

Student records, faculty records, academic information, institutional information and other data submitted or maintained for the College through the App remain under the College's control. The Technical Provider does not claim ownership of institutional records or student personal information merely because it processes or stores that information.

The College is responsible for deciding what institutional information is collected, ensuring an appropriate legal basis, authorising accounts and staff access, maintaining record accuracy, responding to data-rights requests, setting retention periods and obtaining parental or guardian consent where required by law. The Technical Provider will process institutional information only within its authorised technical role and applicable legal obligations.

## 9. Security measures

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards may include role-based access controls, encrypted network communications, secure password storage, session management, authentication controls, rate limiting, security monitoring, restricted administrative access and backup and recovery mechanisms.

No software application, network or storage infrastructure can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorised access.

## 10. Security incidents and data breaches

The Technical Provider will notify the College within the period agreed between the parties after becoming aware of a confirmed security incident affecting College information processed through the App, subject to applicable law. The Technical Provider will provide reasonable technical assistance for investigation, containment and remediation.

The College remains responsible for determining and fulfilling its statutory notification obligations as the relevant Data Fiduciary, where applicable. This allocation does not remove any direct legal obligations imposed on the Technical Provider.
